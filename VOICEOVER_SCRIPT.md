*(Video shows the Bean & Leaf Coffee landing page, hero banner, and feature highlights)*

> **"Welcome to Bean & Leaf Coffee. Today’s web promises AI agents everywhere, but for local neighborhood businesses, expensive cloud servers and per-token API bills make adopting AI prohibitive.**  
> **We built Bean & Leaf to prove that any local shop can deploy a complete, autonomous multi-agent network on the Open Agentic Web—with zero server costs and zero inference fees."**

---

### [0:18 – 0:34] Scene 2: The 5-Agent Roster
*(Video scrolls over the 5 agent cards: Concierge, Hours, Menu, Order, and Reservation)*

> **"Our shop is powered by five collaborative agents: our Coffee Concierge serving as the front host, alongside specialist agents for live store hours, dietary-filtered menus, order ticketing, and table reservations.**

### [0:34 – 0:50] Scene 3: Open Discovery & Manifest
*(Video scrolls down to the manifest inspector, toggling between `orders.json` and `agent-manifest.json`)*

> **"Here is our public `agent-manifest.json`. Because it adheres to the Open Agentic Web specification, external personal assistants like Siri or Otari can automatically discover our capabilities over standard HTTPS, while all incoming tickets stream into an open, verifiable state board."**

---

### [0:50 – 1:04] Scene 4: In-Browser Runtime & BYOK
*(Video clicks into `runtime.html`, showing the BYOK dropdown cycling through Gemini, Anthropic, and Mock)*

> **"Launching into our browser runtime, everything executes directly on the client. Through our Bring-Your-Own-Key model, customers can connect Google Gemini, OpenAI, Anthropic, or local Ollama—or use our zero-setup Mock engine. The shop never pays a penny for inference."**

---

### [1:04 – 1:16] Scene 5: Intent Routing & Dietary Filters
